Hypothesis test — ttl cause claim

Claim under test: "the ttl=120 s change causes the gateway failures."

Test: for one hour on 2026-03-04, the gateway ran with ttl restored to
300 s (a controlled intervention; only the ttl changed, the 2.3
build stayed deployed).

Result: 5 failures occurred during the restore hour,
within the same range as the preceding hours with ttl=120 s.

Conclusion: under this test (restored ttl, same build), the failures
continued, so the claim "ttl=120 s causes the failures" is refuted
under the tested condition. The test does not identify what else causes them;
it only removes this candidate under the stated condition.
