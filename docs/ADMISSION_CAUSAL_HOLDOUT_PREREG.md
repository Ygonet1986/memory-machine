# admission-causal holdout — one-shot evaluation pre-registration

Status: **frozen before the single evaluation**. Date: 2026-09-22. Track:
synthetic lab. The policy S (relation slot, `admission-causal-v2`
exploratory, frozen) is evaluated **once** against B on a **new** fixture
with new rationales, relations, decoys and absence cases.

## 1. Frozen policy S (from `ADMISSION_CAUSAL_V2_EXPLORATORY.md`)

One `justified_by` slot, depth 1, from the top-3 lexical candidates, causal
cue on the raw question (`\b(why|reason|led|because|justification|explain)\b`
plus Portuguese cues), highest-BM25 linked target, provenance recorded, one
item, budget 4000 chars, total <= 3 items, no threshold relief for other
candidates; P3v4 otherwise unchanged. Baseline B = P3v4 over lexical top-5.

## 3. Single evaluation and gates (all required)

Run once with `eval/admission_causal_holdout.py`; append the record; no
further runs of S on this fixture, no re-fit of the policy or constants.

- **H1** availability(S) >= 0.90 **and** > availability(B).
- **H2** precision(S) >= 0.70 **and** >= precision(B).
- **H3** factual control: availability(S) >= availability(B) and precision(S)
  on `factual_control` >= precision(B) - 0.05.
- **H4** absence: availability(S) >= availability(B) on `causal_absent` and
  `undue_rate(S) <= undue_rate(B) + 0.05`.
- **H5** budget/cap respected (<= 3 items; 4000 chars).
- **H6** determinism.

## 4. Outcomes

- **All gates pass**: the relation-slot mechanism is **validated on the
  holdout** (still lab-only evidence). The candidate is prepared for a
  future real window starting from zero - never promoted automatically.
- **Any gate fails**: the mechanism does not generalize; the causal line
  closes for the lab with the record, and no re-fit follows.

## 5. Threats

Template fixtures share design regularities; the holdout is new but still
synthetic. Lab evidence is not product evidence. The exploration round was
mechanistic; this holdout is the first independent in-lab test.
